How to play it

Resonant Spectra is an instrument, not a slideshow. The photographs are the score; the light is grown from them. Everything below changes the performance live.

Mouse

↔
Drag left / right on the pictureShifts the hue of the whole scene — the photo and every wash, plume and beam grown from it.
↕
Drag up / down on the pictureRaises or lowers the intensity of the light.
⟳
Scroll wheelZooms the photograph in and out of the frame.
⧉
Double-clickToggles fullscreen. Controls and cursor fade out after a moment; move the mouse to bring them back.
▦
FilmstripClick any photo along the bottom to jump to its movement.
